Treatment Steps

1
🚨 Remove affected leaves NOW
Pull off all spotted or yellowing leaves. Put them in a bag, not on the ground.
2
💊 Apply fungicide within 2 days
Use Mancozeb or Chlorothalonil. Mix 2g per litre of water. Spray in the evening.
3
💧 Avoid overhead watering
Water at the base only. Wet leaves help the fungus spread faster.
4
🔁 Check again after 5 days
Take a new photo to see if the disease has stopped spreading.
